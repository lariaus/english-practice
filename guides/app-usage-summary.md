# Application Usage

## RecorderLoop

A simple infinite loop of record / replay.
You decide at the beginning whether you want the speaking time to be 5/10/15/30/60, or 90s (same time indefinitely; you need to go back to change it).
This is designed to let you listen to the sound of your own voice as it is, not to shadow / repeat someone.

Pros:

- Full flexibility to say and hear whatever you want, for as long as you want.
- Great for pure / real feedback on your own words and voice.

Cons:

- Feedback only from what you hear / think.
- No reference sound to know if what you're saying is right.

## Robot Shadowing

Shadow a robot voice and compare its pronunciation with yours.
The robot says a sentence, then you repeat it, and hear yourself again.
Can optionally repeat the same sentence 1 or 2 more times before moving to the next one.
Infinite loop, always goes to the next sentence.
Sentences are stored in big JSON files, classified in sections (each section can be like an exercise or difficulty level).

Pros:

- Since it's a robot, there are so many possibilities / themes / durations / difficulty levels to shadow.
- You can use LLMs to quickly generate sentences very specific to your needs and what you're working on.

Cons:

- If the text is automatically generated, it might not be authentic / might not represent how people actually speak (or how you'll speak).
- This is a robotic voice. No matter how good it is, it'll always be missing things like emotion, realness, and variation. This can be an issue depending on the exercise / goals.

## YT Shadowing

Shadow real YouTube videos.
Can record / replay / repeat multiple times.
Compared to other modes like RecorderLoop or Robot Shadowing, where you just choose the initial setting and then it runs in an infinite loop of speak / listen forever without control, here the app is full of options and controls.
That means it's a lot more powerful and allows you to target and work on very specific things.
But it also means you need to put in more conscious / real effort, and it requires you to be in control.

Pros:

- Shadowing real people, can choose who, can control speed, how many times you repeat, how to shadow, etc.
- Can actually hear real English, not just generated or more educational content.

Cons:

- Not a simple speak/listen/speak loop, harder to control and use well.
- Harder to work on something very specific sometimes (e.g. want to work on the dark "l" sound, not easy to find content with lots of dark l).
